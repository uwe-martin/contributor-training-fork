#7 Bring your repository up to date with its upstream#

Option 1 syncs changes of an additional remote to the local repository. Option 2 syncs two remote repositories without involving the local repository. Option 1 is for consuming updates from a repo one does not contribute back to. Option 2 provides the basis for contributing back to the original. In S-CORE, contributors don’t push directly to eclipse-score/* repos. They fork, make changes, and open PRs from their fork.
